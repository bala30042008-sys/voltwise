How the bill is calculated

Each slice of your usage is charged at the rate of the slab it falls in. A unit in the 101–200 slab costs ₹4.70 even if your total is 900 units; the higher rates apply only to the units above each slab boundary.

The first 100 units are free under an assumed subsidy. Your bill is the sum of every slab's units × rate.

These are configurable example rates (edit the SLABS list in app.js). Actual tariffs and subsidies depend on your electricity provider and applicable rules.
